GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Who can join PAHADI?

Anyone who respects the community and wants to connect around Pahadi culture, the Himalayas, music, gaming or friendly conversation.

Is it only for people from Uttarakhand?

No. People from any region are welcome, as long as they follow the server rules and treat others respectfully.

How do I join?

Use any “Join Discord” button on this page to open the PAHADI invite.

Where can I find the rules?

Join the server and check its rules or information channels before participating.
